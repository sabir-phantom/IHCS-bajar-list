1. What this system is

Two web pages that live on your computer as ordinary files. You open them by double-clicking. There is no server, no login, no installation, and no internet needed — the fonts are stored inside the files themselves.

FileWhat it does
order_form.htmlTakes a customer's order, saves it to history, prints the order sheet, and hands the order to the bajar list.
bajarlist.htmlTurns an order into a market shopping list, calculated from recipes, and prints it on your three standard forms.
bajar-data.jsonOptional but recommended. One file holding everything both pages save. See section 7.
old/Earlier versions of the order form, kept only for reference.
Do this once before anything else — set up the data file. Without it, your order history and every change you make live only in this browser, and clearing browsing data wipes them.
  1. Open bajarlist.html, scroll to the bar at the bottom, click নতুন ডেটা ফাইল তৈরি করুন.
  2. Save it beside the HTML files, keeping the name bajar-data.json.
  3. Open order_form.html, and on its bar click ডেটা ফাইল খুলুন and pick that same file.
  4. Both bars should show a green dot. Done — every change now saves to the file.

Needs Chrome or Edge. Full details in section 7.

2. Everyday use

  1. Open order_form.html and fill in the customer, date, venue and headcount (ভি.আই.পি and প্যাকেট).
  2. Pick a সেট মেনু next to the headcount, if this order needs a market list.
  3. Tick the menu items. Checked items move to the top of each block.
  4. 💾 সংরক্ষণ করুন saves the order to history. 🖨️ প্রিন্ট করুন prints the order sheet.
  5. 🛒 বাজার লিস্ট opens the bajar list with this order's headcount, date, venue and set menu already filled in.
  6. Check the quantities, correct anything that looks wrong, then print.
If no set menu is chosen, the 🛒 button refuses and says so. That is deliberate: not every order needs a market list.

3. The bajar list page

Header

Date, venue, event name and লোকসংখ্যা (headcount). Everything is calculated from the headcount: recipes are stored per 100 people and scaled. Type 250 and every amount becomes two and a half times the per-100 figure.

Headcount: VIP and প্যাকেট

Two boxes, not one. Everything is costed on the two added together — 500 VIP + 200 প্যাকেট = 700 — and the line beneath shows that total.

A dish cooked for fewer people gets its own number. Beside each dish in the আইটেম row is a small box showing the total in grey. Type 500 into চিকেন রোস্ট and that dish alone is costed for 500 while কাচ্চি stays at 700; the box turns dark to show it was set by hand. Clear it and the dish follows the total again. Set fresh each job.

An ingredient used by two dishes adds up their separate amounts. সয়াবিন তেল is 3 লিটার/100 for কাচ্চি and 2 for চিকেন রোস্ট: at 700 for both that is 35 লিটার, and with the roast for 500 it is 31.

Taking a percentage off

The সব পরিমাণ কমান box above the list reduces every quantity by the percentage you type — 10 means ten percent less of everything, on the sheets and on the cooks' pages alike.

While a percentage is set, the table grows a পুরোটাই column. Tick an ingredient there and it keeps its full amount while the rest are cut — for the things you never want short. Ticks are cleared for each new job.

The review table

Three tables, one per printed form. Each row has:

ColumnMeaning
দ্রব্যের নামThe item name. Click to rename it.
প্রতি ১০০ জনেThe amount for 100 people. Changing this rescales the order amount.
এই অর্ডারেThe amount for this order. Changing this is a one-off and does not change the recipe.
এককUnit: কেজি, গ্রাম, লিটার, মিলি, পিছ, পাউন্ড.

The ▸ arrow

Every ingredient that comes from a recipe has a small arrow before its name. Click it to see which dishes use that ingredient and how much each contributes:

দুধ ▾
কাচ্চি — ৭৫০ গ্রাম
চিকেন রোস্ট — ২৫০ গ্রাম
রেসিপি অনুযায়ী মোট — ১ কেজি

If you typed your own number into that row, a red line appears saying the handwritten value is being used instead of the recipe total. An ingredient with no arrow does not come from any recipe.

Searching the list

Above the three tables is উপকরণ খুঁজুন. Type part of a name and all three sheets filter at once to the rows that match, filled or empty, with a count beside the box. The bar stays in view as you scroll. ✕ সব দেখান clears it.

Searching only changes what you see. Quantities you type while filtered are saved normally, and the printout is unaffected — every row with a quantity still prints.

Rows with no quantity

They are folded away behind "▸ পরিমাণ নেই — … টি সারি (দেখান)". Click to show them. They never print.

Changing the forms

  • Rename any item, heading or sheet title by clicking the text.
  • Change a unit with the dropdown in the একক column.
  • Add a row or heading with + নতুন সারি / + নতুন উপশিরোনাম. The কোথায় dropdown next to them chooses where it goes: at the top, under any heading, or at the end.
  • Delete rows you added with the red ✕. Original form rows have no ✕ so they cannot be deleted by accident.
  • ↺ ফর্ম আগের অবস্থায় ফেরান at the bottom undoes all form changes.

4. Set menu packages

A package is a named group of dishes, for example স্ট্যান্ডার্ড কাচ্চি প্যাকেজ. The order form's সেট মেনু dropdown lists them.

  • + নতুন প্যাকেজ — give it a name, tick its dishes, tick its dessert options (জদ্দা, ফির্নি, both or neither).
  • ✎ সম্পাদনা — change the selected package.
  • প্যাকেজ মুছুন — only for packages you created. The original one cannot be deleted.

A package with no recipes still works. Create it, type the amounts into the প্রতি ১০০ জনে column, then press 💾 এই সেট মেনুর ডিফল্ট হিসেবে সংরক্ষণ. From then on that package fills itself in.

Giving a dish to another cook (বাবুর্চি)

Every dish in the আইটেম row has a small বাবুর্চি dropdown with three choices: S (standard), A and C. Leave it on S and nothing changes. Set a dish to A or C and its ingredients leave the three normal sheets and print on that cook's own page instead.

  • Nothing is bought twice. If কাচ্চি (S) and বোরহানি (C) both use চিনি, the form prints S's share and Chef C's page prints C's share. The two add up to the full amount.
  • Nothing is mixed. Chef A's page holds only Chef A's dishes; Chef C's page only Chef C's. They are never combined.
  • In the review table a red A or C badge marks every row that goes to a cook, and ফর্মে … next to it says how much stays on the paper form. The full total is still shown in এই অর্ডারে, so the two will differ on a shared item — that is correct.
  • Below the three tables you get a read-only preview of each cook's page. Edit the quantities in the tables above; the preview follows.
The assignment is not saved. Every time you open the page, all dishes start on S. This is deliberate: a forgotten assignment left over from last week would quietly empty items out of your shopping sheets.

5. The recipe library

Below the package box. It holds all 28 dishes. Changing a recipe changes every package that uses it.

  • + নতুন রেসিপি — name the dish, tick "এটি একটি মিষ্টান্ন" if it is a dessert, then add ingredients. Each ingredient picks a form row, an amount per 100 people, and a unit.
  • ✎ সম্পাদনা on any recipe to change it.
  • মূল রেসিপিতে ফেরান undoes your changes to a built-in recipe.
  • রেসিপি মুছুন only appears for recipes you created. Deleting one also removes it from any package that used it, and the confirmation names those packages.

Finding an ingredient, and adding a new one

The ingredient box is a search box. Start typing and the list narrows to matching rows — there are over 200, so this is faster than scrolling. Eighteen names sit on more than one row (घি is on three, ডিম on four); those are listed with their sheet after the name, so you always pick a specific row.

The suggestions are drawn by the page itself, directly under the box. Arrow keys move through them, Enter picks, Escape closes. On a narrow window each ingredient becomes a stacked card — name on its own line, then amount, unit and ✕ — so nothing has to be scrolled sideways to reach.

If the name matches nothing, the box turns pink and offers to create it: choose which of the three sheets it belongs on, choose its unit, and press + নতুন উপকরণ তৈরি করুন. The row is added to that form permanently and selected for this recipe in one step.

More cooks: at the very foot of the page is a small বাবুর্চি তালিকা line. Type a name, press + যোগ করুন, and that cook appears in every dish's dropdown and gets their own printed page under that name. Removing a cook returns their dishes to standard, and says how many first. The list is saved; who cooks what is not.

The red ✕ removes an ingredient line. A line you have filled in asks for confirmation; an empty line is removed straight away.

Why ingredients pick a row rather than free text: every ingredient must land on a row of the printed form, so that two dishes using the same item add up into one line instead of being bought twice.

6. Printing

  • Three sheets print, in this order: পার্টি বাজার লিস্ট, বাজার লিস্ট, সবজী. The review screen lists them in the same order.
  • The first sheet names the dishes it is buying for, on a রান্নার আইটেম line under the header, the same way a cook's page does. Dishes handed to বাবুর্চি A or C are named on that cook's page instead, since their ingredients have left these sheets.
  • Only rows with a quantity print. Empty rows are left out and the numbering runs 1, 2, 3 down the printed rows.
  • Price columns are not printed (দর, টাকা, মোট টাকা) — the buyer writes prices at the market.
  • Long sheets switch to two columns on the same page so a category never spills onto a second page. Past 22 rows it goes two-column; past 56 the text tightens further. All of it was measured against A4.
  • A heading with nothing under it is dropped automatically.
  • If any dish is assigned to বাবুর্চি A or C, the cooks' sheet — titled বাবুর্চির রান্নার তালিকা — prints last, after the three market sheets, so it can be torn off the back of the packet and handed over. It carries the usual তারিখ / লোকসংখ্যা / স্থান / অনুষ্ঠান header, and above each table sits that cook's name and the dishes they are cooking.
  • Each cook gets a page of their own — A first, then C — so a sheet can be handed over without another cook's list on it. Each stays on a single A4 page, using the same rules as the market sheets: one column up to 22 rows, two columns beyond that, and tighter type past 56. Measured against A4: 22 items fill 989px in one column, 56 fill 963px in two, and the ceiling is 106 items at 1020px of the 1032px page — 107 spills. The review screen warns in red from 101, a few items early, because a long ingredient name can wrap onto a second line and cost height.
  • The last tier is small print. Past 56 items the type tightens to fit, and at the 106-item ceiling it is legible but not comfortable in a kitchen. A realistic cook's list is 20–45 items, which stays full size. For scale, the whole ingredient master is 227 rows and the standard কাচ্চি package uses 41.
Print from Chrome or Edge, paper size A4, margins "Default". Do a test print once on your own printer, as printers differ slightly.

7. Your data and backups

There are three layers. Understanding them is the difference between safe and sorry.

Layer 1 — inside the files

The 28 recipes, the form rows and the standard package are written inside bajarlist.html. They survive anything, including a new computer.

Layer 2 — browser storage

Everything you change is saved by the browser on that one computer: recipes you add, packages, saved per-100 values, form edits, your menu library and your whole order history.

This layer is fragile. Clearing browsing data, a Windows reinstall or a different browser and it is gone. That is what layer 3 is for.

Layer 3 — the data file (recommended)

One file, bajar-data.json, holding everything both pages save. Set it up once:

  1. Open bajarlist.html, scroll to the bar at the bottom, click নতুন ডেটা ফাইল তৈরি করুন.
  2. Save it next to your HTML files, keeping the name bajar-data.json.
  3. Open order_form.html and click ডেটা ফাইল খুলুন on its bar, choosing the same file.

From then on every change is written to the file within half a second, and the dot in the bar turns green. When you reopen a page, the browser asks once for permission — click ডেটা ফাইল আবার চালু করুন.

Both pages share the file safely: each one only rewrites its own part. The bajar list owns the recipes, forms, packages and per-100 values; the order form owns your menu library and order history.

Needs Chrome or Edge. Firefox cannot write files this way, so there the pages fall back to browser storage and the manual backup buttons.

Manual backup

Both pages also have ব্যাকআপ ডাউনলোড and ব্যাকআপ থেকে পুনরুদ্ধার links. Useful for keeping a dated copy before a big change. Restoring asks for confirmation first, because it replaces what is there.

8. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
The 🛒 button refuses to openNo সেট মেনু is selected, or no menu items are ticked.
The list is emptyলোকসংখ্যা is blank. Type a headcount.

9. Technical reference

For whoever maintains the files later.

KeyContents

Data file format

{ app: "iqbal-bajar", version: 2, savedAt, per100, forms, setMenus, recipes, orderForm: { menu, history } }

A file whose app is anything else is refused. The file handle is remembered in IndexedDB under database iqbal-bajar, store handles, key dataFile — that holds a pointer to the file, not your data.

Row ids

PrefixSheet
p1…p87পার্টি বাজার লিস্ট, matching the printed form's numbering
n1…n33Extra items added to the পার্টি sheet

r1…r17রুটির মালামাল section of that sheet
u…, rec…, pkg…Rows, recipes and packages created in the app

Recipe format

{ label, dessert?, ingredients: [ [rowId, qty, unit], … ] } where qty is per 100 people and unit is one of kg, gm, ltr, ml, pcs, pound. Older built-in recipes name the row by an alias instead of an id; ROW_ALIASES maps those.

Unit kinds must match. Putting a weight amount on a row whose unit is পিছ multiplies it a thousandfold. The code that checks this caught exactly that bug with বড় পেঁপে (3 কেজি read as 3000 পিছ).

One item, one row

Ghee was written on three different rows across the paper cards (ঘি, গাওয়া ঘি, and the পার্টি sheet's ঘি). All nine dishes that use it now point at the single ঘি row on the বাজার লিস্ট sheet, so an order using several of them prints one combined line instead of two or three. The unused rows stay on the form but print empty.

10. Numbers still to confirm

Transcribed from the handwritten cards, worth checking before a big order:

  • মোতানজান জদ্দা — গোলাপজল and কেওড়া জল are set to 1 bottle each. The card says 150 গ্রাম, but the form counts bottles.
  • মোতানজান জদ্দা — the four spices split at 5 গ্রাম each make 20 গ্রাম, where the card's combined line reads 15 গ্রাম.
  • মুরগ পোলাও — মোরগ ৫০ পিছ uses the দেশী মোরগ row, although "দেশী" was struck out on the card.
  • ডরি ফিস — its card is written for 30 কেজি of fish, which is the amount for 100 people, so it went in as written. দই আড়ং "১ টা" was entered as ১ কেজি.
  • Three handwritten additions on the blank row 88 of the cards could not be read: সাদা পোলাও (250 গ্রাম), মিক্সড ভেজিটেবল (200 গ্রাম), চাটনি (20 গ্রাম).
  • চিকেন রোস্ট and জালি কাবাব carry no chicken, following the master bazaar list. The standard কাচ্চি package therefore buys no মুরগী at all.
